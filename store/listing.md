**Full description** (4000 max):

```
TorqueMedic turns a cheap Bluetooth OBD-II adapter into a complete diagnostic
tool. Read the codes behind your check engine light, watch live sensor data,
understand what failed, and follow a guided repair plan — all from your phone.

DIAGNOSE
• Read and clear stored, pending and permanent diagnostic trouble codes
• Plain-language explanation for each code, with the systems it touches
• Freeze-frame data captured at the moment the fault was set
• Readiness monitors, so you know whether the car will pass inspection

WATCH LIVE
• Live sensor data: RPM, coolant and intake temperature, mass air flow, oxygen
  sensors, fuel trims, throttle position, battery voltage and more
• Vehicle health summary built from the values the ECU actually reports
• Recording and history, so an intermittent fault does not disappear the moment
  you stop driving

REPAIR
• Step-by-step repair guidance tied to the codes that are actually present
• Parts and checks ordered by likelihood, so you are not guessing
• Estimated cost and difficulty for each job
• A service record you can keep with the vehicle

KNOW YOUR CAR
• Read the VIN and vehicle details the ECU reports
• Save several vehicles, each with its own scan history
• Multi-protocol support: ISO 15765 (CAN), ISO 9141-2, KWP2000 and SAE J1850

WORKS WITH WHAT YOU HAVE
• Bluetooth Low Energy, Bluetooth Classic (SPP) and Wi-Fi ELM327 adapters
• No account, no subscription, no data collection — scans stay on your device
• No adapter yet? A built-in demo vehicle lets you try every screen first

TorqueMedic is a diagnostic aid. It is not a substitute for a professional
inspection, and it will not repair hardware faults by itself. Always follow the
vehicle manufacturer's service procedures.
```
