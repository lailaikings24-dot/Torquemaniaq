## Full description

```text
TorqueMedic turns your phone into an OBD-II scan tool. Plug in an ELM327-compatible adapter and it reads what your engine computer already knows — trouble codes, freeze-frame data, readiness monitors and live sensor values — then helps you work out what to do about them.

Not sure yet? Connect the built-in demo vehicle and try every screen before you buy a dongle.

READ AND CLEAR CODES
• Stored, pending and permanent trouble codes, grouped by the module that reported them
• Plain-language explanation, severity and a drive-or-stop verdict for the codes in the repair library
• Freeze frame, readiness monitors and Mode 06 on-board test results
• VIN, calibration ID and CVN where the ECU exposes them
• Clear codes with Mode 04, then re-scan to confirm the repair

LIVE DATA
• Gauges, trends and a compact live view for the sensors your car supports
• Choose which PIDs to watch and keep that set
• Log a session to CSV and review it afterwards

REPAIR ASSISTANT
• Ranked likely causes and an ordered diagnostic path, not just a code number
• Parts to check and typical cost bands
• A repair log per code, with printable reports you can keep or hand to a mechanic

MADE FOR REAL ADAPTERS
• Bluetooth LE and Bluetooth Classic in the app
• Bluetooth LE in the browser
• Wi-Fi or WebSocket bridge for adapters that expose a TCP port
• Custom PIDs (Mode 22) with header and formula for manufacturer-specific ABS, body and transmission data
• An adapter terminal for AT commands when a dongle misbehaves

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
No account, no ads, no analytics. Diagnostic data stays on your device, and CSV files or reports are created only when you export them. Clear everything from Tools whenever you like.

HONEST COVERAGE
Powertrain data follows the OBD-II standard: US petrol from 1996, EU petrol from 2001, EU diesel from 2004. ABS, airbag, body and other manufacturer modules answer only on their own protocols — that is what custom PIDs are for. An ELM327-compatible adapter is required and is not included.
```

## Claims to avoid

Play rejects listings that overpromise, and reviewers check screenshots against
the text. Keep these out:

- "works on every car" — say OBD-II compliant powertrain data, with the year
  ranges above;
- "diagnoses any problem" — the app explains codes and guides checks, it does not
  replace a mechanic;
- "free" as a headline when a paid adapter is required — say the adapter is sold
  separately;
- "best", "#1", star ratings or fake urgency;
- medical, legal or safety guarantees.

## Developer contact details in Play Console

- **Developer name:** the name shown on the listing (your account name or
  `TorqueMedic`).
- **Email:** `privacy@torquemaniaq.com`, or the support alias above.
- **Website:** the deployed URL until the custom domain is live.
- **Phone:** optional — only publish a number you are willing to support.
- **Address:** Play requires a developer address for some account types, and EU
  DSA "trader" status asks for contact and address details. Fill in what your
