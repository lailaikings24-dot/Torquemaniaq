Privacy Policy

TorqueMedic · Effective 12 November 2026 · Applies to the Android and iOS apps and the web version

TorqueMedic collects no personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no server that receives your vehicle's information. Everything the app reads from your car stays on your device.

What the app reads, and where it goes

TorqueMedic talks to an OBD-II adapter over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. It reads diagnostic trouble codes, freeze-frame data, live sensor values and the vehicle identification number (VIN), and sends the commands needed to request them. Those readings are written only to local storage on your phone or computer so the app can show history and repair guidance. They are never uploaded, sold or shared.

When no adapter is connected, the app can run its built-in demo vehicle. The demo data is simulated on your device and has nothing to do with a real car.

Permissions and what they are for

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_SCAN, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT on Android; the Bluetooth usage descriptions on iOS) — used only to discover and communicate with your OBD-II adapter.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) — requested on Android 11 and older only, because the Android operating system requires it to scan for Bluetooth devices. On Android 12 and newer the app requests Bluetooth permission with the neverForLocation flag. TorqueMedic never reads your GPS position, never derives location from Bluetooth scans, and never stores or transmits location data.
  • Internet — used to load the app's own interface and, if you open it, this policy. No diagnostic data is sent anywhere.

Data stored on your device

Saved vehicles, scan results and app preferences are kept in your browser or WebView's local storage. You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the app. Because nothing is sent to us, there is no server-side copy to request or delete.

Third parties

The app contains no advertising networks, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, and no social plugins. It does not use cookies for tracking. Distributing through Google Play or the App Store means those stores may collect their own installation and diagnostic information under their own privacy policies; that is outside this app's control.

Children

TorqueMedic is a tool for vehicle owners and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Your vehicle's data

Diagnostic data can include the VIN, which identifies a specific vehicle. Because it stays on your device, you remain in control of it: delete it by clearing the app's data, or by disconnecting the adapter.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app adds a feature that changes any of the above — for example optional cloud backup — this page will be updated before that feature ships, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy can go to support@torquemaniaq.com.

TorqueMedic is a diagnostic aid, not a substitute for professional inspection or repair. Always follow the vehicle manufacturer's procedures.
